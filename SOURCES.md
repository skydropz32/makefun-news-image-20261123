# Sources — Monday, 23 November 2026

Every story in `entry.png` was first reported or confirmed on Monday,
23 November 2026. Each fact and figure below was verified against the primary
source named before it was drawn. The full research brief is kept outside
this repository.

## 1. Nvidia: Huang returns as CEO, shares soar 31% (lead)
- Reuters, 23 Nov 2026 — "Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, shares
  soar": board reinstated Huang unanimously, effective immediately, 11 days
  after his 12 Nov resignation; Nasdaq +6%, S&P 500 +4%.
- CNN, 23 Nov 2026 — shares closed +31.48%, best day since 2003, market
  value up ~$952 billion.
- The Wall Street Journal, 23 Nov 2026.

## 2. Ukraine sinks Russian corvette off Libya
- CNN, 23 Nov 2026; Reuters, 23 Nov 2026; AP (via TVNZ), 23 Nov 2026.
- Ukraine says it sank the corvette Merkury at Benghazi with seven naval
  drones launched from a "mothership"; a Ka-27 helicopter was destroyed and
  the cargo ship MV Angara was hit. The attack was early Sunday 22 Nov; the
  claim was announced and confirmed on Monday 23 Nov by President Zelenskyy
  and the SBU. Libyan officials described the ship as severely damaged, so
  the image says "Ukraine says it sank".

## 3. Hormuz on edge: Iran vows to fight the US blockade
- Reuters, 23 Nov 2026 (world summary + US Treasury sanctions report).
- Tasnim / Mehr, 23 Nov 2026 — Iranian Army says it shot down a US "Lucas"
  drone over the Persian Gulf east of Hormuz; IRGC said on Sunday it downed
  a US MQ-1 Predator drone.
- Middle East Eye, 23 Nov 2026.
- Iran's foreign ministry (spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei) said on Monday Iran
  would use all tools to confront the US naval blockade; the US Treasury
  sanctioned 20+ Iranian shadow-banking entities on Monday. The number of
  tankers hit by mines is reported inconsistently (2 vs 3), so the image
  names no tanker count.

## 4. NATO sends AWACS to Poland
- Reuters, 23 Nov 2026 — "NATO to send early-warning system aircraft to
  Poland after missiles incident": announced by Polish deputy defence
  minister Pawel Zalewski after a NATO meeting in Brussels; SACEUR General
  Alexus Grynkewich authorised the forward deployment. Three Russian Kh-101
  missiles landed in Poland on Friday 20 Nov. NATO's AWACS fleet: 14 aircraft.

## 5. EU proposes 22nd sanctions package on Russia
- Reuters, 23 Nov 2026; Statement by President von der Leyen, European
  Commission, 23 Nov 2026 16:35 CET.
- ~60 Russian banks face a transaction ban and removal from SWIFT; a crypto
  sanctions task force; 70+ shadow-fleet ships listed; a ban on
  Russian-insured tankers; a visa ban for Russian ex-combatants; Russian
  teams and athletes banned from competing under the Russian flag at EU
  sports events.

## 6. India pledges $1.62 billion to Nepal
- Reuters, 23 Nov 2026; AFP, 23 Nov 2026; The Hindu BusinessLine, 23 Nov 2026.
- PM Narendra Modi announced a 240-billion-Nepalese-rupee ($1.62 billion)
  package after talks with PM Balendra Shah in New Delhi: a $377 million
  grant plus a $1.24 billion line of credit; eight MoUs signed. The 26 August
  glacier-collapse flood killed ~1,500 people, with 5,200 missing.
